WHY SUPPORT NDEN

Investing in inclusion.

Nyatike Disability Empowerment Network works to champion the rights, inclusion and independence of persons with disabilities within Nyatike, Migori County.

Support can help strengthen community-based efforts, advocacy, capacity building and other areas connected to NDEN's work.

HOW TO SUPPORT

Let's discuss the best way to contribute.

NDEN's official information does not currently publish payment details on this website. To ensure that support is directed appropriately, please contact the organisation directly.

The NDEN team and other partners can provide guidance, materials and services on appropriate ways to support the organisation's work including support to learning institutions for learners and trainees with disabilities. support may include: school feeding programmes for learners with disabilities, provision of school uniforms, school and college fee payments.

Other support can include: assistive devices, modified teaching and learning materials, modified school infrastructure and adapted sanitary facilities.

In addition, children and learners with disabilities should be identified and assessed for school placement by Educational Assessment and Resource Centres (EARCs) service providers.

Advocacy and campaigns should be organised for all children, learners and trainees living with disabilities to be registered with National Council for Persons with Disabilities (NCPWD) so as to benefit from affirmative action.
