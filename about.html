About NDEN

Empowerment begins with inclusion.

Nyatike Disability Empowerment Network brings people together to champion the rights, inclusion and independence of persons with disabilities within Nyatike, Migori County.

Who We Are Our Mission

Who We Are

A network built around people.

Nyatike Disability Empowerment Network is a registered Community Based Collaborative Coalition uniting individual groups, advocates and service providers to collectively champion the rights, inclusion and welfare of Persons with Disabilities.

The Network focuses on collective advocacy, capacity building and coordination of services strategically aimed at empowering targeted individuals living with disabilities.

Community-led People with disabilities, families and allies working together.

Why We Exist

Stronger together, more included.

NDEN was formed to unite people with disabilities, families and allies so they can secure equal rights, share vital resources and achieve full community inclusion.

The Network is grounded in lived experience, with a focus on overcoming societal barriers and improving access to support and services.

Our Approach

Development starts with people.

NDEN uses a participatory, self-sustainable, community-based and volunteer-based approach.

The Network uses a case management approach that puts beneficiaries and members of the community at the centre of their own development.

Through this approach, people gain livelihood skills, financial literacy and capacity to become resilient and self-sufficient.

Our Direction

Purpose that guides our work.

OUR MISSION

Connect. Motivate. Empower.

Our mission is to connect, motivate and provide a platform for people with disabilities to build confidence, develop independence and sustainable social and professional lives, and fully participate in every aspect of life.

OUR VISION

A community where voices are valued.

To build a community of empowered and highly networked persons with disabilities whose voices are heard and valued.

Our Goal

Championing rights, inclusion and independence.

The major goal of Nyatike Disability Empowerment Network is to champion the rights, inclusion and independence of persons with disabilities within Nyatike, Migori County.
