OUR PROGRAMMES

Programmes that create possibilities.

NDEN's programmes bring together advocacy, skills, support and community action to advance the rights, inclusion and independence of persons with disabilities.

WHAT WE DO

Turning community priorities into practical action.

Nyatike Disability Empowerment Network works through nine programme areas designed to support participation, empowerment, rights, education and independence among persons with disabilities.

The programmes reflect NDEN's community-based and participatory approach, placing people at the centre of their own development.

PROGRAMME 01 Rights & Voice

PROGRAMME 01

Human Rights Advocacy and Civic Education

Championing the rights of persons with disabilities while supporting civic awareness and participation.

The programme works toward strengthening the voice and autonomy of people with disabilities and promoting awareness of disability rights and responsibilities.

Advocacy rights to persons living with disabilities to access basic, quality, inclusive and equity education.

Advocacy to access quality and barrier free health care.

Advocacy and support for persons living with disability against gender based violence.

Advocacy for people living with disabilities to be treated with respect, non discrimination, equality and equity in all activities undertaken in the communities they live in or stay.

PROGRAMME 07 Education & Opportunity

PROGRAMME 07

Education Support and Linkages

Remove stigma among the parents or care givers of children with Disabilities as well as removing stigma among children and other Persons living with disabilities.

Promote and advocate for educational assessment to children, learners and trainees for learning and training institutional placement as well as promoting medical assessment for children and other Persons living with disabilities for registration with National Council for Persons with Disabilities (NCPWD).

Linking learners and trainees living with disabilities with institutions, organisations, well wishers and government agencies to access educational funding support.

Support children, learners, trainees and other Persons living with Disabilities in education during pandemic and other disasters.

PROGRAMME 08

HIV and Aids Sensitization and Support Services

Promoting awareness, acceptance and resilience among People with Disabilities infected and affected by HIV and Aids.

This programme supports NDEN's commitment to ensuring that People with Disabilities are not excluded from essential information, support and community participation.

PROGRAMME 09

Adapted Co-Curricular Activities

Promoting participation of children and learners with disabilities in adapted co-curricular activities as part of inclusive education and community participation.

The programme provides opportunities for learners with disabilities to participate in activities that support social interaction, confidence, talent development and inclusion.

Through participation in adapted co-curricular activities, learners can develop their abilities, interact with others and take part more fully in school and community life.
