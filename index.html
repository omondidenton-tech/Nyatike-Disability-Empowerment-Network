Our Approach

Development starts with people.

NDEN is participatory, self-sustainable, community and volunteer based. Its case management approach puts beneficiaries at the centre of their own development.

Through this approach, community members gain livelihood skills, financial literacy and capacity to become resilient and self-sufficient.
