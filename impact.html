OUR IMPACT

Progress begins with action.

NDEN is building the foundations for stronger disability advocacy, inclusion and community empowerment within Nyatike, Migori County.

DOCUMENTED PROGRESS

Building a stronger foundation for change.

NDEN's current work focuses on understanding the disability community and strengthening the framework needed to advance human rights advocacy and civic education.

The organisation's documented achievements reflect work currently underway rather than unsupported claims of completed large-scale impact.

CURRENT ACHIEVEMENTS

Two important steps already underway.

01

COMMUNITY MAPPING

Mapping and Enlisting the Disability Community

NDEN is currently mapping and enlisting the disability community within Nyatike, Migori County.

This work contributes to a stronger understanding of the community and helps create a foundation for coordinated advocacy, support and inclusion.

02

STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK

Human Rights Advocacy and Civic Education Framework

NDEN is currently developing a strategic framework for Human Rights Advocacy and Civic Education covering the period 2026–2028.

The framework is intended to provide direction for continued advocacy and civic education work within the organisation's area of focus.

OUR APPROACH People at the centre

HOW CHANGE IS BUILT

Impact starts with people and participation.

NDEN uses a participatory, self-sustainable, community-based and volunteer-based approach.

Through a case management approach, beneficiaries and members of the community are placed at the centre of their own development, with a focus on livelihood skills, financial literacy, resilience and self-sufficiency.

OUR DIRECTION

Working toward lasting inclusion.

NDEN's work is guided by its goal of championing the rights, inclusion and independence of persons with disabilities within Nyatike, Migori County.

01

RIGHTS

Stronger Voices

Supporting people with disabilities to build confidence, direct their own lives and participate in decisions affecting them.

02

INCLUSION

Greater Participation

Working toward communities where stigma, discrimination and barriers to participation are challenged.

03

INDEPENDENCE

Building Capacity

Supporting access to skills, education, opportunities and essential support that can contribute to greater independence.

04

COLLABORATION

Working Together

Bringing people, groups, advocates and service providers together to collectively champion disability rights and welfare.

BE PART OF THE JOURNEY

There is more work ahead.

NDEN is continuing to build its advocacy, community support and partnership efforts. Your involvement can contribute to a more inclusive community.
